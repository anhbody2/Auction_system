# gds-003 goods buyer column and resale

SRS: System - update goods table, Req-1, Req-2 (high priority).

## Behaviour

- `goods.buyer_id` (nullable FK to users) records who won the goods.
- Settlement (`settleAuction`, be/src/lib/settlement.ts) sets `seller_id` = the auction's seller and `buyer_id` = the
  winner in the same transaction as the payment and payout. On a first sale the seller does not change; on a resale
  the previous buyer becomes the seller (confirmed reading of "update buyer --> seller", 2026-10-21).
- No winner (no bids, reserve not met) or a cancelled auction leaves both columns as they were.
- Owner = `buyer_id ?? seller_id`. Goods state is now computed for the viewer (`goodsState(goods, viewerId)`):
  - viewer is not the owner (the former seller) -> `sold`
  - owner with an upcoming/active auction -> `in_auction`
  - otherwise `available` (a buyer's goods also carry `bought: true` and show the badge "bought")
- My goods (`GET /api/me/goods`) lists goods the user sells/sold or bought. After a resale the first seller is neither,
  so the goods leave their list (their past auction stays in My auctions).
- Creating an auction for goods you sold is now **403** "These goods have been sold" (was 409), as the gds-003
  verification asks for 404/403. Goods in an open auction stay 409; goods you never owned stay 404.
- Edit/delete of sold goods by the former seller stays 409 "Sold goods cannot be changed" (gds-002). The owner (buyer)
  can edit or delete goods they bought while they are not in an auction.

## Migration

`be/prisma/migrations/20261021090000_goods_buyer`: adds the column and FK, then backfills from each goods' last
auction that ended with a winner (seller = that auction's seller, buyer = its winner). Checked by
be/tests/goods.buyer.test.ts, which runs the backfill statement from the migration file.
